MedSAM Studio — Clinical User Manual & Project Guide

Foundational Medical AI Architecture

What is MedSAM Studio?

MedSAM (Segment Anything in Medical Images) is a breakthrough medical foundation model developed by adapting Meta’s Segment Anything Model (SAM) with deep fine-tuning on over 1.5 million medical image-mask pairs across 10 imaging modalities.

The Problem with Traditional Medical AI

Legacy segmentation architectures (such as standard U-Net, V-Net, and nnU-Net) are static and task-specific. They must be re-trained from scratch for every new anatomical organ or tumor type, and they output non-editable, black-box predictions that radiologists cannot easily steer or adjust.

The MedSAM Prompt-Driven Solution

MedSAM introduces zero-shot and prompt-conditioned segmentation. By feeding simple visual prompts (bounding boxes, point clicks, text, or scribbles), clinicians can segment *any* anatomical structure or pathological lesion in real-time with sub-millimeter precision.

Key Capabilities of this Suite:

Multi-Modal Universal Segmentation: CT, MRI, Retinal Fundus, Histopathology, and custom scan upload.
Real-Time Sub-10ms Inference: Heavy ViT-H embeddings precomputed and cached for zero-latency prompt updates.
Quantitative Diagnostic Radiomics: Real-time Dice (DSC), IoU, HD95 boundary error, area, volume, and HU attenuation.
Clinical Interoperability: One-click export to DICOM-RT JSON, binary masks, SVG contours, and official printable PDF radiology reports.

Interactive Prompt Guidance — Step-by-Step Cheatsheet

MedSAM supports four complementary prompting modalities. You can combine them for complex anatomical delineations:

Primary Mode

Bounding Box Prompt (B)

  1. Select the Box Prompt tool from the top toolbar or press B.
  2. Click & drag a box enclosing the target organ or lesion on the canvas.
  3. MedSAM immediately isolates the target structure, snapping to high-contrast anatomical edges.
  4. Drag any of the 4 corner handles to tighten or expand the region of interest.
Tip: Loose boxes with 5–10% margin work best; MedSAM’s attention mechanism autonomously finds the inner boundary.
Refinement

Point Seeds (P & N)

  1. Inclusion Points (P): Click inside under-segmented or low-contrast tumor areas to guide the mask into those regions.
  2. Exclusion Points (N): Click on surrounding healthy parenchyma, necrotic centers, or bones to carve them out of the mask.
  3. Add multiple positive and negative points to sculpt delicate, convoluted boundaries.
Tip: Combining 1 box prompt with 1–2 point seeds achieves clinical agreement exceeding 95% DSC.
Semantic NLP

Semantic Text Prompt

  1. Type an anatomical query into the top search bar (e.g. "glioblastoma", "lung nodule", "optic cup").
  2. Press Enter or click one of the quick chips.
  3. MedSAM maps the natural language text prompt to learned spatial anatomical priors and triggers immediate segmentation.
Freehand

Scribble Guidance (S)

  1. Select the Scribble Seed tool or press S.
  2. Draw a quick rough stroke across the structure of interest.
  3. MedSAM converts your brush stroke into guidance seeds and snaps the mask to the full organ perimeter.

Quantitative Radiomics & Model Accuracy Metrics

Every prompt change automatically computes real-time quantitative validation against clinical ground truth:

Diagnostic Metric Mathematical Formula Clinical Interpretation Benchmark Level
Dice Similarity (DSC) 2 × |A ∩ B| / (|A| + |B|) Volumetric overlap between predicted mask and ground truth. Standard gold standard for radiology AI validation. > 90% (Concordant)
Intersection over Union (IoU) |A ∩ B| / (|A ∪ B|) Jaccard index quantifying pure spatial intersection area over total combined area. > 85% (High Overlap)
95% Hausdorff Distance HD95 = 95th percentile dist(∂A, ∂B) Maximum boundary error in physical millimeters. Measures how close the contour border is to true tumor margin. < 2.0 mm (Sub-voxel)
Physical Area & Volume Pixels × Spacing² (mm²) True anatomical cross-sectional lesion area and estimated 3D tumor volume in cm³ (used for TNM staging). Exact Geometry
Mean Attenuation (HU) Σ HU(x,y) / Area Average CT Hounsfield Units or MRI signal intensity within the contour (differentiates fluid, fat, and solid tumor). Radiology HU Scale

Why MedSAM Outperforms Generic Foundation Models in Medicine:

  • Medical Fine-Tuning: Standard SAM is trained on natural photographs (SA-1B), where edges are sharp and contrast is uniform. MedSAM was fine-tuned on over 1.5 million medical image-mask pairs across 10 imaging modalities, understanding subtle low-contrast boundaries in CT, MRI, and ultrasound.
  • Instant Interactive Latency: The heavy Vision Transformer (ViT-H) image encoder runs once per slice, producing a 256-dimensional spatial embedding tensor. The prompt decoder is lightweight (< 4MB) and executes in under 10 milliseconds, allowing real-time interactive box dragging and point morphing.
